Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions

I offer a comprehensive range of creative services including photography, brand identity design, web design and development, creative direction, and digital marketing. Each service can be customized to meet your specific needs.

Project timelines vary based on scope and complexity. A basic logo design typically takes 2 weeks, while a complete brand identity with website can take 4-8 weeks. I'll provide a detailed timeline during our initial consultation.

I offer both package pricing and custom quotes. Package pricing is transparent and outlined on my pricing page. For custom projects, I provide detailed quotes after understanding your specific requirements. A 50% deposit is required to begin work.

Yes! I work with clients worldwide. Most of my communication happens via video calls, email, and project management tools. For photography projects, travel can be arranged if needed.

The number of revisions depends on your package. Starter packages include 2 revision rounds, Professional packages include unlimited revisions, and Enterprise packages are fully customizable. Additional revisions can be purchased if needed.

To begin, I'll need information about your business, target audience, project goals, and any existing brand materials. I'll send you a detailed questionnaire after our initial consultation to gather all necessary information.

Yes! I offer various support and maintenance packages for websites and ongoing brand development. We can discuss your specific needs and create a support plan that works for you.

You'll receive all source files in industry-standard formats. For design work, this includes AI, PSD, PDF, PNG, and JPG files. For photography, you'll receive high-resolution JPG files and RAW files upon request.
