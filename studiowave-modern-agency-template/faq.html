FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

GENERAL

What services do you offer?

We offer comprehensive branding and identity design, digital marketing strategy, social media management, web design and development, content creation, SEO, and creative strategy consulting.

What industries do you work with?

We work across diverse industries including technology, e-commerce, healthcare, finance, hospitality, and consumer goods. Our experience spans B2B and B2C brands.

Do you work with startups?

Absolutely! We work with businesses of all sizes, from early-stage startups to Fortune 500 companies. We offer flexible packages to fit different budgets and needs.

PRICING & BUDGET

What is your pricing structure?

We provide custom quotes based on project scope, complexity, and timeline. Most branding projects start at $10,000, web design at $15,000, and marketing retainers at $5,000/month. Contact us for a detailed proposal.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes, we offer flexible payment plans for larger projects. Typically, we require 50% upfront and 50% upon completion, with milestone-based payments for extended engagements.

What's included in your pricing?

Our pricing includes strategy, design, revisions (typically 2-3 rounds), project management, and final deliverables. Additional services like photography, video production, or ongoing support are quoted separately.

PROCESS & TIMELINE

How long does a typical project take?

Timelines vary by project type: Branding (6-8 weeks), Web Design (8-12 weeks), Marketing Campaigns (4-6 weeks). Rush projects can be accommodated with adjusted pricing.

What is your process?

We follow a 5-step process: Discovery (research & strategy), Strategy (planning & concepts), Design & Execution (creation), Launch (deployment), and Growth & Optimization (ongoing improvement). Learn more

How involved will I need to be?

We value collaboration and will need your input at key milestones: kickoff, strategy review, design presentations, and final approval. Expect 3-5 hours of your time per week during active phases.

TOOLS & DELIVERABLES

What tools do you use?

We use industry-standard tools including Adobe Creative Suite, Figma, Webflow, WordPress, Google Analytics, HubSpot, Hootsuite, and various marketing automation platforms.

What file formats will I receive?

For branding: AI, EPS, SVG, PNG, JPG. For web: HTML/CSS, design files (Figma/Sketch). For marketing: Source files, final assets, and comprehensive brand guidelines.

Do I own the final work?

Yes, upon final payment, you receive full ownership and rights to all deliverables. We retain the right to showcase the work in our portfolio unless otherwise agreed.
